## Speed

| Full run (249 repositories, 20 shards of 13)                                     | Wall time   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Before: 40144503063                                                              | 59 min 48 s |
| After, balanced shards, cold caches: 40166506002 (#309, refit-bench `l2-speed`)  | 28 min 22 s |
| After, balanced plan reused, warm caches: 40168273227                            | 32 min 48 s (13 shards queued up to 453 s for runners other runs held; about 26 min without that wait) |

With balancing, the plan job assigns repositories longest first from the previous run's measured seconds: shards then took 1,020 to 1,650 s (median 1,404 s), and all 249 outcomes match 40144503063. With warm caches, 104 of 104 baseline checks are reused (baseline check time 1,716 s → 241 s), and the summed benchmark time falls 7.8% more. Dependency bundles are reused for 23 of 106 original lockfiles, because a run saves at most 12 GiB of bundles (2.6 GB compressed) under refit-bench's 10 GB cache limit; a larger limit (an org admin setting) would keep more. Before, the slowest shard (3,545 s) set the wall time; the per-repository sum is 25,018 s, about 1,251 s per shard if balanced. `scripts/benchmark-merge.mjs` (#309) reproduces the merge job's `merged` artifact for 40144503063 byte for byte (all 8 files, checked by the master on the shard artifacts).
