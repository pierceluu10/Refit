<objective>
Re-read a seeded random 10% of the label candidates independently, measure agreement on `live` and `via`, adjudicate every disagreement by reading the code again, and record all of it in the labels header (BENCH-02; D-15, D-17, with D-07, D-09, D-10, D-14, D-16 kept). The re-read is made by one separate fresh-context agent that the orchestrator launches at the Task 2 checkpoint, given only the sample and the rules. The executor draws the sample, then computes agreement and adjudicates.

Purpose: Phase 4's traced fraction rests on these labels. A measured agreement rate and recorded adjudications say how far to trust them.
Output: sampling and agreement helpers with tests, two new modes of the label script, the updated labels file and a BENCHMARKS.md sentence.

Commits: conventional messages (`feat(03-03): ...`, `test(03-03): ...`, `docs(03-03): ...`) with no Co-Authored-By trailer and no other AI attribution. Stage explicit paths only. | Adjudications -> labels file | Changes to committed labels |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-14 | Repudiation | independence of the re-read | high | mitigate | The sample is drawn from candidate keys only, and `--reread-sample` cannot open labels. | T-03-15 | Tampering | silent label edits during adjudication | medium | mitigate | Only `--reread-merge` writes the file. Each change needs an adjudication entry with a note, recorded in `reread.disagreements`, and the validators re-run |

| T-03-17 | Information disclosure | code text in adjudication notes | low | mitigate | Notes are capped at 160 characters, single line, and rejected when they contain the source line (D-16) |
