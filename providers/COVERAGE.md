# Coverage: one live change per change kind

F6 of the 2026-11-08 reliability night. For every change kind in MVP-PLAN.md section 2, one change a Refit watcher read from a live source, with its link and the watcher's quote, or a plain "none yet" and why. Nothing here is padded or made up.

**Where each row comes from.** "Scan" rows are from real scans of the refit-dev demo repos on 2026-11-08 (`f1-scan.ts` on `mvp` at f67cbaaf, Sonnet 5.5 watchers). "Recorded read" rows are the platform watchers' saved live reads in `providers/platforms/fixtures/live/<group>/`, recorded on prompt platform-watch-v3. Every quote passed the watcher's own quote check (`submit_changes` refuses a quote it cannot find in the fetched page). The **Re-checked** column is a second, independent check made while writing this: a plain fetch of the URL on 2026-11-08 and a search for the quote. "not confirmed" means that fetch got a different rendering (a PDF-backed EUR-Lex record, a page that needs a browser), not that the quote is wrong.
