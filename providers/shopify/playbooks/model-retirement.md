Shopify doesn't retire AI models. This kind rarely applies here. For retired platform features (Scripts, checkout.liquid), see product-shutdown. For data-model changes such as the 2024-04 product model, see rest-to-graphql and field-removed. Version retirement is covered by api-version ([versioning](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/usage/versioning)).
