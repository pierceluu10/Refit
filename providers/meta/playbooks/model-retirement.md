# Model retirement

Rarely applies to the Meta Graph/Marketing API, which does not expose versioned ML models to developers. The closest analogues are retired Insights metrics (`field-removed`) and whole product shutdowns (`product-shutdown`). Check `meta_lookup_field` and `meta_search_docs` if a notice looks like this.
